Home Care Questions? Ask Us Anything!

What East Valley families ask most before their first in-home care visit.

What happens at the free consult?

We sit down with you at home and talk through the care you need, the days that work, anything we should know, and how you'd like to pay. It costs nothing and there's no commitment.

Can we book just one visit?

Yes. One visit, with no package and no contract. Families book us before a family event, or for the week their usual helper is away.

Which cities do you cover?

San Tan Valley, Queen Creek, Gold Canyon, Apache Junction, Gilbert, Chandler and Mesa. Just outside those? Call and ask, and we'll tell you straight.

Does insurance cover in-home care?

Usually not. Custodial care like bathing isn't covered by Medicare, Medicaid or most commercial insurance. A private long-term care policy may reimburse you or pay us directly, and if you already qualify for the VA's Aid & Attendance benefit, we'll look at that with you.

Do you offer full-time caregiving?

No. We focus on bathing, incontinence and personal care visits so we can do them really well, on the days you choose.

How does billing work?

Weekly or monthly, by check or by card through secure online invoices.
